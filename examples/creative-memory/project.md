# Creative Memory

## Problem statement

Performance marketing teams struggle to retain and apply learnings from high
volumes of advertising creative, causing repeated mistakes and wasted ad
spend.

## Solution idea (parked — do not let it leak into the problem)

A shared library that tags every creative with its results and surfaces past
learnings when briefing a new one.

## Decision to make

Is the pain of losing creative learnings strong enough that a specific team
would change how they work or pay to fix it?
