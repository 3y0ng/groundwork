# Interview guide — H3 (consequence is severe) × S1 (DTC performance marketers)

## 1. Context

- Walk me through your role and how creative testing fits into your week.
  _Establishes volume and ownership before probing pain._
- Who else touches creative decisions — briefs, approvals, buying?

## 2. Most recent occurrence

- Tell me about the last time a learning from a past creative got lost or
  ignored. _One specific, dated instance beats a general impression._
- When was that? What was going on at the time?

## 3. Current workflow

- Walk me through exactly what happened, step by step, from brief to
  catching the repeat.
- What do you use to keep track of what's been tried?

## 4. Severity & consequences

- What did that episode cost you in time or money? _The core of H3 — push
  for a number or a concrete downstream effect._
- What happened because of it — internally or in the account?

## 5. Existing alternatives

- What are you using today to avoid repeats? How did you end up with that?

## 6. Previous attempts

- What have you already tried to fix this? Why did that not stick?

## 7. Spending & resources

- What do you currently pay for in the creative-ops area? Who signed off?

## 8. Decision-making

- Last time your team adopted a new tool, how did that decision actually get
  made?

## 9. Commitment / next step

- Could you show me the sheet or database you mentioned?
- Who else should I talk to about this?
