# Performance marketers at DTC brands

## Workflow

Briefs and ships dozens of creatives per week across Meta and TikTok; reviews
performance in ad manager and a spreadsheet.

## Trigger event

A previously winning creative fatigues and CAC spikes.

## Existing alternative

A shared Google Sheet of "winners" plus tribal memory.

## Why they'd care

Directly measured on CAC and ROAS; repeated creative mistakes cost them money
and time.

## Why they might not

May feel their current spreadsheet is "good enough".
