# The learning-loss problem is real

## Belief

We believe performance marketers at DTC brands lose the learnings from past
creative when briefing new ones, causing them to repeat creative approaches
that already failed. We will treat this as supported when we observe at least
4 of 7 relevant participants describe a specific past repeat-mistake.

## What would prove this wrong

Participants remember their learnings fine, or ship too little creative for
it to matter.

## Evidence required

A concrete story of repeating a creative mistake because the learning was
lost.

## Assumptions

- Teams ship enough creative for memory to matter
- Learnings are not already captured elsewhere

## Consolidation

_2026-08-02, over 4 relevant conversations across 2 segments._

**Conclusion: partially_supported.** The problem is convincingly real —
but only where creative volume is high. Both high-volume DTC teams gave
dated, specific accounts of repeating an already-failed creative (EV-1-1,
EV-4-1), both maintain failing workarounds (EV-1-4, EV-4-5), and both backed
their words with concrete next steps (EV-1-6, EV-4-7). The one low-volume
founder flatly does not have the problem and named why (EV-3-1: a handful of
creatives a month). This is behaviour-heavy support, not enthusiasm: zero of
the supporting weight comes from opinions or compliments.

- **Supporting**: EV-1-1, EV-1-4, EV-1-6, EV-2-1, EV-4-1, EV-4-3, EV-4-5,
  EV-4-7
- **Contradicting**: EV-3-1 (low-volume founder; memory suffices)
- **Unclear**: EV-3-2 (compliment — worth zero; his "bigger teams would love
  it" is speculation, not evidence)
- **Patterns**: repeat-mistakes appear exactly where weekly creative volume
  is 30+; every affected team built a tracking workaround that decayed
  (sheet, Notion).
- **Outliers**: Tom B. — the observable trait is volume, not attitude.
- **Common triggers**: CAC spike surfacing a fatigued/known-bad creative.
- **Common workflows**: brief → ship high volume → review in ad manager +
  spreadsheet.
- **Common consequences**: wasted spend on re-run duds ($6k; CAC +30%).
- **Existing alternatives**: shared Google Sheet; self-built Notion DB
  (abandoned); tribal memory.
- **Existing spending**: Motion (~$400/mo at Loop; also at BrightCart),
  Foreplay.
- **Commitment strength**: two real commitments — an intro to a budget
  holder and two peer intros — both volunteered.
- **Unanswered questions**: where is the volume threshold at which the
  problem starts to bite? Do mid-volume teams (10–30/week) feel it?
- **Recommended strength**: strong (computed above). The conclusion is
  "partially" only because support concentrates in S1 — consider narrowing
  the hypothesis to high-volume teams rather than claiming it for marketers
  broadly.
