# Insights

## Pain concentrates at high creative volume

- level: project
- date: 2026-08-02

Everyone who ships 30+ creatives a week feels this; the founder running a
handful of creatives a month does not, and says why. The real segment may be
"high-volume DTC performance leads", not "performance marketers" broadly.

## Frequency is proven, urgency is not

- level: hypothesis
- ref: H3
- date: 2026-08-02

The problem is clearly frequent, but only two participants named a real
quantified consequence ($6k; CAC +30%). Do not let the strong frequency
signal masquerade as urgency.

## The denial may be a measurement artefact

- level: interview
- ref: 2
- date: 2026-08-02

Devin's "no cost I noticed" came with "we do not track that". Teams without
creative-level attribution may have the same waste and no way to see it —
worth asking every future participant how they would even know.
