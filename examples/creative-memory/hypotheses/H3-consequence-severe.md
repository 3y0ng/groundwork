# The consequence is severe enough to act on

## Belief

We believe the wasted spend and repeated mistakes are painful enough that
teams would change their workflow. We will treat this as supported when we
observe 4+ participants name a real, sizeable consequence.

## What would prove this wrong

Teams shrug it off; the cost is invisible or absorbed.

## Evidence required

A quantified or clearly felt consequence of the lost learning.

## Assumptions

- Wasted spend is attributable and visible

## Consolidation

_2026-08-02, over 3 relevant conversations in 1 segment._

**Conclusion: inconclusive.** Two participants gave quantified, dated
consequences ($6,000 wasted; CAC +30% for three days) — real cost, the
strongest kind of urgency evidence. But a third participant from the same
segment, who confirms the problem *happens* weekly, flatly denies felt cost
and ranks the problem below other headaches (EV-2-2, EV-2-4). This is not a
majority-vote situation: two accounts of real spend outweigh one shrug, but
they do not yet clear the bar the founder set (4+ named consequences), and
Devin's "in a way I noticed — we do not track that" hints the divide may be
*measurement*, not pain.

- **Supporting**: EV-1-2 ($6k on a re-run dud), EV-4-2 (CAC +30% × 3 days)
- **Contradicting**: EV-2-2 (no cost he noticed), EV-2-4 (not top-three)
- **Unclear**: none
- **Patterns**: quantified consequences come from teams with daily CAC
  monitoring; the denial comes from a team that does not track this.
- **Outliers**: Devin K. — possible explanatory trait: no attribution/
  tracking of creative-level waste.
- **Common triggers**: CAC report spikes.
- **Common consequences**: wasted spend, days of elevated CAC.
- **Existing spending**: consequences land as ad-spend waste, not tooling.
- **Commitment strength**: no urgency-specific commitments yet; Alina's
  pilot interest is conditional (EV-4-6, booked under H4).
- **Unanswered questions**: how often does the *costly* class of repeat
  recur per team? Do teams without daily CAC reporting ever notice? Is
  Devin's shrug representative or an artefact of not measuring?
- **Recommended strength**: mixed (computed above). This is the thinnest
  load-bearing hypothesis: the founder's core decision hinges on it, and the
  next interviews should aim squarely at consequence (see guide G1).
