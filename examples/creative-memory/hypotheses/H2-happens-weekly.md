# It happens often enough to matter

## Belief

We believe DTC performance marketers face this at least weekly because of the
volume of creative they ship. We will treat this as supported when we observe
4+ participants describe it recurring weekly, tied to real cadence.

## What would prove this wrong

It comes up only occasionally, or only during rare campaign launches.

## Evidence required

Frequency described as weekly or higher, tied to real cadence.

## Assumptions

- Creative volume is high and sustained
